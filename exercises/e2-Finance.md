# Exercise 2: Finance

Explore Copilot usage, license assignments, cost center ownership, and budgets. Complete each step in your Enterprise account and review how the choices affect cost visibility.

## 1. Overview

1. Go to your **Enterprise account**.
2. Select **Billing & Licensing**.
3. Open **Overview** and review the available Copilot usage and billing information.

## 2. Licenses

1. In **Billing & Licensing**, open **Licensing**.
2. Select **Manage**, then **Assign licensing**.
3. Assign a Copilot license to yourself.
4. Assign a license to your neighbor.

## 3. Cost center

1. In **Billing & Licensing**, select **Cost centers**.
2. Select **New cost center**.
3. Name it `UniverseCostCenter-USERNAME`, replacing `USERNAME` with your own name so you can find your exercise cost center.
4. Choose **Organization** as the resource type, then select your organization in the enterprise.
5. Check the checkbox for **AI credit included usage cap** to limit how many included AI credits this cost center can use. If the credits run out, choose whether to stop further use or allow paid overage.
6. Create the cost center.

Choose the resource scope that matches what you want to track:

- **Enterprise teams** can group members by a team whose costs you want to track.
- **Organization** gives you a broad view of costs associated with that organization.
- **Users** can help track costs for specific people, such as power users.
- **Repositories** can help attribute repository-based usage, such as GitHub Actions used by automation-heavy projects.

## 4. Budgets

1. Open **Budgets and alerts** and select **New budget**.
2. Choose a **product-level** budget.
3. Select **Copilot** as the product, then configure the budget.
4. Under Budget scope select the cost center you created earlier.
5. Set the budget amount to **$100** for this exercise.
6. Add another member as a budget alert recipient so multiple users are notified when the budget approaches its limit.

## 5. Review

1. Return to **Overview** and review the Copilot usage and billing information.
2. Check the license assignments, cost center, and budget you created.
3. Confirm the cost center includes the organization you selected and the budget alert recipient is listed.
4. Consider what each setting lets Finance see or manage, and what you would explain to the budget owner.

## What to explain to Finance

- **Overview:** "I can see how much Copilot is being used and what it costs, so Finance has a starting point for understanding spend."
- **Licenses:** "I can assign licenses to the people who need Copilot, so access and license costs are tied to actual users."
- **Cost centers:** "I can assign Copilot costs to an organization, team, or specific users, so we can see which part of the business is responsible and plan or compare spending."
- **Budgets:** "I can set a $100 budget for Copilot AI credit usage and notify another budget owner, so the people responsible can keep track of spend and respond when it approaches the limit."
- **Your recommendation:** "I used usage data, license assignments, a cost center, and a budget to make Copilot spending visible, assign ownership, and give budget owners a way to monitor it."
