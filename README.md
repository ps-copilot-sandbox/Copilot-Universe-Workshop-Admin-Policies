# Copilot Universe Workshop: Admin Policies

Enterprise administrators are increasingly expected to explain Copilot adoption, spending, and governance to leadership, finance, and security. This interactive workshop puts participants in the role of a Copilot administrator responding to three realistic stakeholder inquiries.

Rather than focusing on theory, the session is a practical walkthrough of administrative settings, organizational data, and the decisions that shape Copilot management. Participants will explore each scenario with the group and then work through it on their own laptop.

## Workshop scenarios

The workshop opens with a Monday-morning leadership question: **How do we constrain Copilot, and what do we have in place to manage its use today?** From there, participants work through:

1. **Leadership — adoption and usage:** Assess how Copilot is being used and explain the controls available to manage that usage.
2. **Finance — spending and ownership:** Understand where Copilot spend comes from, how it maps to the organization, and who owns it.
3. **Security — policies and access:** Review how policies, access, and administrative controls are managed across the enterprise.

Each scenario combines a guided review of the admin experience with hands-on practice. The workshop closes by bringing the findings together into a practical action plan focused on adoption, spending, and governance.

## What participants will take away

- A repeatable approach to answering executive questions about Copilot.
- Practice navigating administrative settings and interpreting organizational data.
- A clearer understanding of the scope of Copilot controls and the decisions they support.
- An action plan for making informed administration decisions across the enterprise.
